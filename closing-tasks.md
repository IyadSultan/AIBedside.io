    <h2 id="b10-cart-title">An intelligent crash cart</h2>
    <p class="demo-stage-copy">Every tool in this hour has a place on a cart. Voice. Weight-based doses. Step-by-step guidance. A camera that times the team. Training mode when the ward is quiet. This is a concept drawing, not a product you can order. The six jobs are already in your notes.</p>

      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Calculate doses</p>
        <p>Weight in. Dose out. The math is not the job during a code.</p>

      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Guide actions</p>

      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Evaluate the team</p>
        <p>Camera-assisted timing and feedback after the event.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Run mock training</p>
        <p>Scenarios, scoring, and debrief when the corridor is quiet.</p>

  <section class="b10-cart-block" aria-labelledby="b10-guide-title">
    <p class="demo-stage-kicker">And at the clinic desk</p>
    <h2 id="b10-guide-title">Generate intelligent patient instructions</h2>
    <p class="demo-stage-copy">A protocol is written for the team. A family needs a guide they can take home. Load the treatment plan. A person checks it. Put it in the language this patient actually uses. Print. This is a concept drawing, not a product. You already practiced this when you turned a guideline into something a parent can read.</p>

      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Import protocol</p>
        <p>Load the treatment plan. Do not type it from memory.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Review &amp; approve</p>
        <p>A person on the care team signs off before it leaves the clinic.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Personalize</p>
        <p>Clear language for this patient, not a photocopy of the protocol.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="b10-job" role="listitem">
        <p class="k">Print &amp; share</p>
        <p>A take-home guide. Paper still works.</p>
      </article>
