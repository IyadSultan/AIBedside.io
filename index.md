<h2 class="aib-section-title">What you will leave with</h2>
<ul class="aib-objectives">
  <li>Brief a model instead of searching it &mdash; role, context, constraints, format.</li>
  <li>Use tools that show sources, then open the sources.</li>
  <li>Capture your unit&rsquo;s house style as a reusable skill.</li>
  <li>Know that a connector inherits your access &mdash; that is the point and the risk.</li>
  <li>Keep the routine and the sensitive local; use large remote models for the hard problems.</li>
  <li>Walk out with one Monday task for your role. <span class="optional">Never paste patient-identifiable data into a consumer AI tool.</span></li>
</ul>
