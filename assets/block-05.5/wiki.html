Febrile neutropenia

febrile-neutropenia.md · POL-CW-01 v3.1 · Infectious Diseases · 0–18 years

Rules

  • Fever = 38.3°C once, or 38.0°C for one hour.
  • Neutropenia = ANC below 500. Come to ER the same evening. No paracetamol first.
  • Door-to-antibiotic 60 minutes. Peripheral culture first. Pip-tazo (cefepime if rash). Not ceftriaxone.
  • Two named caregivers only while neutropenic.

Open questions

  • Review due March 2026. The discharge leaflet was required to match this file and does not.

Central venous access devices

central-line.md · POL-CW-02 v2.0 · Vascular Access · under 16 years

Rules

  • Neutropenia for line decisions = ANC below 1000.
  • Saline flush. No heparin on PICC/Broviac — except the same file sends every line home with a heparin lock.
  • Skin prep: chlorhexidine 4% aqueous, no alcohol.
  • Fever: notify attending within 2 hours. Line culture first. Vancomycin + cefepime.

Open questions

  • Ages 16–18 have no line protocol in this folder. 4% aqueous CHG may be the wrong product.

Visitor rules and protective isolation

visitor-isolation.md · POL-CW-03 v1.8 · IPC · review overdue

Rules

  • Visiting 10:00–20:00. Well adult visitors, no cap. No visitors under 12.
  • Protective isolation if ANC below 500. Spill kit in dirty utility cupboard B.
  • Influenza visitors: stay away until 3 days after the fever starts.

Open questions

  • Unlimited adults vs two named caregivers in POL-CW-01. Influenza window looks short.

Hazardous drugs (chemotherapy) safety

chemo-safety.md · POL-CW-04 v4.0 · Pharmacy + Nursing · 0–21 years

Rules

  • Closed system. Hold a cycle if ANC is 1000 or lower unless the protocol says otherwise.
  • Night fever after an infusion: day unit in the morning if the child looks well.
  • Doxorubicin extravasation: warm compress (flagged as an error).
  • Spill kit: Room 12 clean utility — not the dirty utility.

Discharge and after-hours fever advice

discharge-fever.md · POL-CW-05 v1.4 · Nursing Education · ID has not signed

Rules families are told

  • ANC = “white cells plus the bands” (flagged as an error).
  • 38.0°C and well: paracetamol, clinic next working day. Fever alone can wait.
  • In ER: antibiotic within 90 minutes; usual drug ceftriaxone.
  • Every line goes home with a heparin lock.

Open questions

  • This is the highest-risk file: it is what parents take home, and it undoes POL-CW-01.
