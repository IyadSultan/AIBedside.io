# Visitor rules and protective isolation

Source: [[../policies/POL-CW-03-visitor-isolation.md|POL-CW-03]] v1.8 (3 Jun 2023) · Owner: IPC · Ages **0–18** · **Review overdue** (due Jun 2025)

Teaching copy. Not KHCC policy. Not for real patients.

## The rules as written

- Visiting hours 10:00–20:00. Overnight only with the nurse in charge, single room.
- Neutropenic child (ANC < 500): **well adult visitors, no cap on numbers**. No visitors under 12.
- Surgical mask for every oncology visitor. N95 not required.
- Protective isolation: single room preferred, positive pressure preferred, no plants, spill kit and isolation gowns in **dirty utility cupboard B**.
- Influenza visitors: stay away until **3 days after the fever starts**.
- Measles exclusion 21 days if not immune. Varicella until crusted.
